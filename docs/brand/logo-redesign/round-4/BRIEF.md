# Atelier mascot: character brief (round 4)

## What Atelier is
A self-hosted code home for one person: your repositories, history, issues and checks, on a machine you own.
"Atelier" = a maker's workshop (artist, couture, alchemist). Git = save points (commits), branches, merges.

## Who the mascot is
The **studio familiar**: a small, original, anime-style (chibi) companion that lives in your workshop,
keeps your history safe and works late with you. Not a servant or a robot: a cosy creature you'd want on a sticker.

Personality: curious, loyal, a little sleepy, quietly proud of the work. Warm, never corporate.

## Visual rules
- Chibi proportions: big head, small body, large eyes with two highlights, small blush marks.
- Atelier palette only: amber `#f2b85c`, deep amber `#c98a2e`, cream `#f6efe3`, ink `#131211`, plus one blush `#e98b6d`.
- Flat shapes, no gradients or shadows; works on the dark app background *and* on white.
- One defining feature that carries the meaning (atelier + git), readable at 24 px.
- Needs a **head-only icon cut** for the favicon/app icon.

## Avoid (category clichés)
Robots, octopuses/cats (GitHub), owls, gophers (Go), crabs (Rust), penguins (Linux), foxes and tanukis (GitLab),
dinosaurs, whales (Docker); slimes and fire spirits from well-known anime/games. Original characters only.

## Longlist (one line each)
1. **Moth familiar** – a fluffy lamp-moth; the first computer "bug" was a moth (1947), and moths gather at the lit studio window at night. Wing eyespots = commits.
2. **Bobbin sprite** – a living thread spool; its sewing needle sticks up like an anime cowlick (ahoge), and its thread trails off with knots for commits.
3. **Flask sprite** – an alchemist's flask with an amber potion that has a face; bubbles rising from the cork split and rejoin like branches.
4. Ink imp – a drop of ink with nib horns.
5. Snail carrying a workshop as its shell – "your home, on your own hardware" (risk: slow).
6. Paper-crane familiar – crafted, folded.
7. Tool golem – a little golem of chisels and brushes.
8. Hermit crab with a studio shell (rejected: Rust's crab).
9. Candle spirit (rejected: too close to a famous anime fire demon).

**Shortlist:** 1 Moth, 2 Bobbin, 3 Flask: three different creatures, each tying atelier to git in one detail.
